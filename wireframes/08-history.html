History: browsing past days

History is not a separate screen: it is the same day view stepped backwards. Past days are read as they were, and the two panes stay in place.

  1. Stepping back with [ changes the day pane. The strip says how far back you are and offers one key home.
  2. A past day shows what was planned and what got closed, with close times. Focus marking is kept, so "what did I decide mattered that day" is visible.
  3. Unfinished tasks on a past day are the review pile, seen from the other side. They can be dealt with here too (close, pull to today, backlog) with the same keys.
  4. When browsing the past, the backlog pane gives way to a day list: a quick scan of done counts, and a way to jump. Days with nothing planned are skipped. l back on the day pane restores the backlog.
Future days] from today steps forward. A future day shows already-moved tasks and scheduled copies that will be created, greyed, so "what is planned for Monday" is answerable. Adding to a future day is allowed.
