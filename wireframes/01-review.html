Morning review, step 1: the pile

Shown on the first open of a work day when the pile is non-empty. Full window, replacing the panes: the review is a ritual, not a sidebar. If the pile is empty the step is skipped silently.

  1. The strip becomes a step indicator. Escape exits without handling anything; the pile stays and the main screen shows "in review" as an alert until it is dealt with.
  2. Grouped by the day the task was planned for, newest first. Old groups are labelled relatively ("3 weeks ago") so age is obvious without arithmetic. Nothing is carried over automatically, however old.
  3. Cursor row. Handled rows stay in place, struck through, showing what happened, so the list does not jump. Order of handling is free.
  4. The four outcomes from PRODUCT.md plus "move to a day" (a superset of "move onto today"). Same keys as everywhere else in the app.
  5. Progress and undo. Undo covers the last handled task; deletes never confirm.
  6. Leaving with items still on the pile is allowed. They are not nagged about again until the next morning, but the alert count stays visible.
Recurring copies on the pile"Write standup notes" is an unfinished copy from Monday. It is handled like any other task; the schedule keeps producing new copies regardless.
