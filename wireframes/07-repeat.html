Recurring tasks

  1. A copy on a day is an ordinary task with a repeat chip. Close, move, reorder, focus: all as usual, none of it touches the schedule.
  2. R on any task (copy or not) opens the schedule card. On a copy it edits the schedule behind that copy; the current copy stays as it is.
  3. The card previews the next dates so "1st of the month" and "every 2 weeks" are unambiguous before saving.
  4. Schedules are listed at the bottom of the backlog pane, collapsed by default, so there is somewhere to see and edit all of them without inventing a fourth "place" for tasks.
What a schedule does each morningBefore the review runs, a fresh copy is placed at the end of that day's plan (not focus). Copies missed while the machine was off are created for those past days, so they land on the pile like any other unfinished task.
