Scratchpad

A post-it block beside the lists, not a notes app. n opens the drawer as a third pane; the backlog gives way to make room, and the day list narrows.

  1. Notes are cards, newest first, showing their first lines and a thumbnail of any ink. No titles, no folders, no search. Independent of tasks by design.
  2. Create, open, throw away. Delete has no confirmation, only the undo toast. Nothing expires on its own.
  3. An open note takes the right pane. The header shows when it was made so a stale note is easy to spot and bin.
  4. Text and freehand ink share the surface. Text flows from the top; ink goes anywhere on top of it. Tools are modes, switched by key, never a toolbar to click through. Pen works with mouse or stylus.
Pop outo detaches a note into its own small floating window (Omarchy's "pop" window rule: rounded, on top). Useful for a message draft while the main window is tiled behind an editor. Closing the float returns it to the drawer.
