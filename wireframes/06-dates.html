Dates on backlog tasks, and waiting

  1. d on a backlog row opens the date card for "due by". r opens the same card for "remind on". A task can carry both; they are shown as separate chips.
  2. One card, two modes; the header says which. Switching mode keeps the typed date.
  3. Three ways in: typed text ("30 sep", "mon", "+3"), the numbered quick picks, or the calendar. Clearing is a pick, not a separate action.
  4. w toggles waiting and moves the row into the Waiting group at once. No reason text: the product treats it as a flag. Waiting tasks keep their dates but only reminders surface them, never the due nag.
Due vs remind, in the reviewDue: surfaces every morning from the date on, with "n days over". Remind: surfaces once, on the date. Both keep the task in the backlog until t pulls it.

Dates are only offered in the backlog. A task on a day already has a date: its day. Moving a dated backlog task to a day keeps the chips, so the deadline stays visible.
