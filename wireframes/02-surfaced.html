Morning review, step 2: surfaced

Backlog tasks whose date has arrived. Surfacing is a prompt, not a move: a task stays in the backlog until pulled with t. If nothing surfaced, this step is skipped.

  1. Due-by tasks: shown from the due date until closed or re-dated. Overdue ones say how far over they are.
  2. Remind-on tasks: shown on that date only. "Keep" on a reminder means it will not be seen again unless the date is changed.
  3. Waiting tasks are not nagged about, so a waiting task with a reminder is shown but dimmed, and never appears in the due group.
  4. Recurring copies created for today are listed for information only. They are already on the plan; no action needed here. This is the one place the app explains what it did on its own.
  5. Decisions are optional. Nothing here blocks starting the day.
  6. Primary action ends the review and lands on Today (screen 03).
When both steps are emptyThe app opens straight to Today. The review is never an empty ceremony.
