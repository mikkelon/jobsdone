Narrow tile (half screen or less)

Omarchy tiles windows; the app will often live in a half-width column beside an editor. Below a width threshold the two panes collapse to one, switched by a tab strip.

  1. Tabs replace panes. Same keys: h/l or tab switch tabs instead of panes. Counts keep the other tabs informative. The notes drawer becomes a tab.
  2. Rows drop their text metadata first ("←backlog", dates in chips become glyph-only) and keep the glyphs. Titles get the width.
  3. Hint bar shows the top five keys and defers to ? for the rest.
Pull from backlog when narrowl to the Backlog tab → t on a task → it moves to Today; the row disappears from Backlog and the Today tab count increments. No confirmation.

The review takes the whole window in both layouts; in narrow mode the action list becomes a popup card on ⏎ instead of a side column.
