Empty states

Empty states say what the list is for and name the one or two keys that fill it. No illustrations, no encouragement.

A, B The two lists point at each other: an empty day suggests the backlog, an empty backlog reminds that days can send tasks back.

C The review only ever appears with content. Its absence is the good news.

D Past days with nothing planned still exist in the day stepper so the calendar stays continuous, but the day list on screen 08 skips them.

E The most likely reason for a miss is that the task does not exist yet, so the empty search offers to add it.

F The new-note card is the whole empty state.
